How to Attach

Four ways to put the charms on a beanie. Work on the cuff or fold first, where the knit is double and holds the weight best.

Push-button rivetFor #01 Pearl rivets

Press-fit pearl rivet

  1. Mark the spots with the beanie on a head form, so the layout sits right when worn.
  2. From the inside, push the metal rivet base through a gap in the knit. Use the hole-punch tool only on tight or felted knits.
  3. Line up the pearl on the post from the outside and press firmly until it clicks or sits flush. On firm bases, a light tap with a small hammer helps.
  4. Tug gently to check it is secure.
Tip · Rivets are semi-permanent and leave a small hole if removed. Test on one spot first, and scatter them unevenly for a "dusted with snow" look.
Safety pinFor #02 · #07 · #16 · #17 Brooch pins

Pin-back brooch

  1. Choose a focal point, e.g. the front cuff beside the logo, or near the top between the ears.
  2. Open the pin and pass it through two or three knit loops so the bow does not sag.
  3. Bring the pin back out and close the clasp. On a cuff, catch only the outer layer so the pin is hidden inside the fold.
  4. Straighten the bow so it faces forward on the display.
Tip · Fully removable with no marks, so it is the easiest way to refresh a beanie between campaigns.
Prong-back pinsFor #03 – #06 Crystal studs

Crystal prong stud

  1. Place the stud on the outside where you want it; the cuff or between the ears catches the light best.
  2. Push the metal pins on the back straight through the knit, between stitches so the yarn is not cut.
  3. From the inside, fold each pin flat towards the centre of the stud. Use flat pliers or the back of a spoon to press them down.
  4. Run a finger over the inside to check no sharp ends stick out.
Tip · Comes in pairs. Mix round, teardrop and rectangle shapes with the pearls for a jewelled look; if a pin end feels sharp, cover it with a small felt dot.
Tack pinFor #08 – #15 · #18 Hearts, pearl buttons & flowers

Tack pin with clutch back

  1. Take the metal clutch back off the pin.
  2. From the outside, push the pin straight through the knit between stitches.
  3. On the inside, press the clutch back onto the pin until it clicks and grips firmly.
  4. Wiggle the charm gently; if it spins, push the clutch a little further up the pin.
Tip · No glue or tools and fully removable. Mix the heart colours and grey / champagne / blue pearls with the crystals for a playful candy look.
